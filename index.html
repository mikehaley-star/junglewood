Our Legacy

Where It All Began

Junglewood was more than a place — it was a world unto itself. With nine greenhouses, fields stretching toward the Rocky Mountains, horses in the corral, and a family big enough to fill every corner, it was the setting for a childhood that could never be replicated.

In 2001, a simple email between siblings sparked something extraordinary: a collaborative retelling of every prank, every adventure, every character who passed through Junglewood's gates. What started as a few nostalgic notes became nine chapters of vivid, hilarious, and heartfelt memories.

This site preserves those stories — along with a family tree spanning over 9,000 names — so that future generations will know where they came from and the spirit of the place that shaped us all.
